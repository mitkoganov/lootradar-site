About LootRadar

LootRadar is a small, independently run site that tracks daily deals on PC and console games, plus gaming hardware like keyboards, mice, headsets, monitors and prebuilt PCs. We manually check each deal before it goes on the site — no auto-posted junk.

Who runs this

LootRadar is run by a small independent team based in Bulgaria. We're gamers first, and we built this site because we were tired of digging through a dozen tabs every time we wanted to know if something was actually on sale.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer — at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the site running and ad-light. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.
