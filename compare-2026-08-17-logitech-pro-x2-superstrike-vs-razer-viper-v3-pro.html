Logitech G PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE

  • + Haptic Inductive Trigger System feels genuinely different from any microswitch mouse
  • + Tunable actuation + rapid trigger reset — big for Valorant/CS2 players
  • + POWERPLAY 2 support for truly wireless charging
  • − 7 g heavier than the Viper V3 Pro
  • − Reviewers flag the side buttons as feeling cheaper than Razer's
  • − Full feature set needs Logitech G HUB installed

Razer Viper V3 Pro

  • + One of the lightest flagship wireless mice on the market at 54 g
  • + Slightly longer battery life, up to 95 hours
  • + Gen-3 optical switches: 90M clicks, zero debounce delay
  • − No haptic feedback or adjustable actuation — fixed switch behavior
  • − No charging dock or wireless-charging pad option included
  • − Small, symmetrical shape suits claw/fingertip more than palm grip

Our take

If you want the newest click technology and play titles where tunable actuation actually matters — Valorant, CS2, Apex — the PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE's haptic switches are the more interesting upgrade, and you're paying for that R&D plus a few extra grams. If raw weight and battery life are what you chase, or you play claw/fingertip grip, the Viper V3 Pro remains the class leader nearly two years after launch, and that longevity says a lot on its own. On rating and popularity they're statistically tied at 4.6★ each — this one comes down to grip style and whether adjustable click feel is worth more to you than the lightest possible mouse.
